## Reproducibility policy

Two distinct checks are mandatory:

1. **Artifact identity.** Frozen manifests, locks, code checkpoints, and evidence files
   retain exact byte hashes. Their SHA-256 is the scientific provenance key.
2. **Numerical equivalence.** Independently recomputed floating-point analytic values
   are compared under preregistered absolute and relative tolerances justified by scale,
   conditioning, oracle disagreement, and a safety multiplier. Cross-version bitwise
   equality is not scientific evidence and is not required.

Every Phase-II confirmatory artifact records the pinned environment descriptor, Python
and NumPy versions, platform, float type, RNG family, tolerances, and exact locked input
hashes. The locked reference registry remains byte-identical; clean recomputation may
differ in final bits but must be componentwise within the frozen tolerances and retain
the same qualitative strata and guards.

## Breadth decision rule

Phase II stops at one of:

- **U1:** a broader class spanning at least three structurally distinct systems,
  including a non-Fisher class and different semantics/temporal structure, supports a
  preregistered shared no-refit law with explicit boundaries;
- **U2:** transport succeeds inside a precise Fisher/geometric island and fails in a
  controlled non-Fisher class;
- **U3:** different system groups require incompatible transported laws, yielding a
  correspondence lattice rather than a global normalization.

Each proposed edge must earn eight fields: state map, native geometry, ideal tangent,
realized estimator, scope assumptions, transported prediction, failure boundary, and
falsifying observation. Structural distance is recorded independently from result.
