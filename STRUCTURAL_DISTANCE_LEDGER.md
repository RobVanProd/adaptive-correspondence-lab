# Structural-distance ledger

This ledger prevents agreement among close relatives from being counted as broad
unification. The machine-readable source is
[`STRUCTURAL_DISTANCE_LEDGER.json`](STRUCTURAL_DISTANCE_LEDGER.json).

## Phase-I classes

| Class | Estimator and geometry | Semantics and time | Evidential weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fixed-reward categorical | Exact mapped fields; entropy/categorical-Fisher/Shahshahani geometry | Learning/evolution/optimization labels on one finite simplex; no environment dynamics | One tightly coupled island |
| Gaussian rank-mu | Finite-sample score/rank estimator; Gaussian Fisher | Continuous distribution optimization at one frozen state | Positive control inside the Fisher-natural family |
| Contextual-bandit plug-in NPG | Finite-sample empirical-Fisher pseudoinverse; block categorical Fisher | Expected-reward policy learning with contextual sampling but no transitions | Cross-estimator transport, still Fisher-natural |

Phase I therefore contains meaningful estimator and state-space distance, but no
non-Fisher geometry, inference semantics, or genuinely sequential environment. Its
cross-class result is real but cannot establish a broad adaptive-system theory.

ACL-006 remains in the contextual-bandit plug-in NPG class. Its role is to explain and
predict the ACL-005 support boundary; regardless of its verdict, it does not increase
the count of structurally independent adaptive classes.

## Candidate ordering after ACL-006

1. **Minimal Bayesian state estimation:** highest semantic gain—belief inference and
   sequential observations—with exact comparators still available.
2. **Non-Fisher mirror descent:** isolates geometry with little implementation surface.
3. **Frequency-dependent evolution:** introduces endogenous state-dependent fitness.
4. **Tiny tabular MDP:** introduces transition-dependent visitation and temporal credit.

This order is provisional. ACL-006's support theory determines which prior quantity is
eligible for no-refit transport and therefore which next class has the highest
information value.
