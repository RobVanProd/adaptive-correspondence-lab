## Phase-I classes

| Class | Estimator and geometry | Semantics and time | Evidential weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fixed-reward categorical | Exact mapped fields; entropy/categorical-Fisher/Shahshahani geometry | Learning/evolution/optimization labels on one finite simplex; no environment dynamics | One tightly coupled island |
| Gaussian rank-mu | Finite-sample score/rank estimator; Gaussian Fisher | Continuous distribution optimization at one frozen state | Positive control inside the Fisher-natural family |
| Contextual-bandit plug-in NPG | Finite-sample empirical-Fisher pseudoinverse; block categorical Fisher | Expected-reward policy learning with contextual sampling but no transitions | Cross-estimator transport, still Fisher-natural |

Phase I therefore contains meaningful estimator and state-space distance, but no
non-Fisher geometry, inference semantics, or genuinely sequential environment. Its
cross-class result is real but cannot establish a broad adaptive-system theory.
